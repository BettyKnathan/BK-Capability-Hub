1. Big Ideas

These are the ideas that connect the Learning Design, Change Adoption, Executive Coaching and Prompt Library pages into one larger capability story.

Judgment Line

Knowledge ≠ Judgment

AI can provide information, summaries and options. Humans still own interpretation, trade-offs, ethics, context and final judgment.

Not Content Conversion

Learning starts with performance

SME content is only the input. Learning design begins when we ask what people need to do, decide, practice and sustain.

Capability First

Start with “do differently”

The stronger design question is not “What content do we have?” but “What must people do differently after this experience?”

Change Is Human

Adoption is not attendance

People need awareness, desire, knowledge, ability and reinforcement before transformation becomes real behaviour.

AI + Human

AI supports; humans decide

AI can extract, compare, draft and structure. Humans diagnose, prioritize, validate, coach and approve.

Future Workforce

Speed is not wisdom

As AI makes information faster, judgment, meaning, reflection and discernment become more valuable.

3. Human + AI Operating Principle

The strongest AI workflows preserve human judgment while reducing avoidable manual effort.

AI should help with
  • Extracting key points from source material
  • Comparing old and new processes
  • Structuring scattered inputs
  • Drafting first-pass outputs
  • Identifying gaps, inconsistencies and questions
  • Creating review-ready starting points
Humans should own
  • Diagnosis and problem framing
  • Change impact and adoption risk analysis
  • Learning strategy and learner fit
  • SME accuracy validation
  • Accessibility, tone and cognitive load
  • Final approval and judgment
Betty Lens: The most valuable AI workflows are not replacement workflows. They are judgment-preserving workflows that improve review readiness.
