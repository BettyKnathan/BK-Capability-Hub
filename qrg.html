Learning Designer Operating Guide

Purpose of this guide

This guide organizes the end-to-end learning project lifecycle into a practical operating model for Learning Designers. It integrates project governance, stakeholder engagement, learner profile, high-level design, detailed design, development, reviews, copy edit, quality assurance, accessibility and CPE/CE documentation.

Learning Designer Operating Guide
This page is a working guide for planning, designing and reviewing learning projects. It does not replace current internal CPE/CE requirements, QA standards, accessibility guidance, brand/copy edit standards or reviewer decisions.

Stage 1

Project Intake

Initial capture of the learning request, known context and starting stakeholders.

Project Intake

Participants

  • LSA
  • LPS
  • LD / LXD
  • Learning Consultant
  • Business sponsor or requestor

Learning Designer activities

  • Review the assigned request.
  • Review existing course or source materials if available.
  • Understand known business drivers.
  • Identify known stakeholders and missing inputs.

Deliverables

  • Project record
  • Initial stakeholder list
  • Request details
  • Known inputs and open questions
Stage 2

Kickoff

Align the project team on process, cadence, business needs and stakeholder roles.

Kickoff Meeting

Participants

  • LSA
  • LPS
  • LD / LXD
  • Learning Consultant
  • Business representatives
  • Lead SMR and optional additional SMEs

Activities

  • Explain the Design Studio process.
  • Confirm ownership and roles.
  • Agree cadence and review approach.
  • Clarify business requirements and the reason for creating/updating learning.
  • Identify Lead SMR and additional SMRs.

Deliverables

  • Project charter
  • Stakeholder map
  • Meeting cadence
  • Governance model
  • Lead SMR identified
LPS note: LPS is involved in almost all project meetings and weekly status calls, except detailed SME knowledge-transfer or content working sessions unless required.

Stage 4

High-Level Design

Translate discovery into learning objectives, topic structure and recommended design approach.

🤖 Recommended Agent
Learning Objectives Generator

High-Level Design

Activities

  • Define measurable learning objectives.
  • Map key topics under each objective.
  • Identify possible activities.
  • Identify possible assessment approach.
  • Recommend modality and tools.

Milestone gate

  • Business signoff required.
  • Confirm HLD with business sponsor or lead business contact before detailed design.
  • Capture feedback and decisions clearly.

Deliverables

  • High-level design
  • Learning objectives
  • Topic outline
  • Assessment approach
  • Modality recommendation
Stage 5

Detailed Design

Build the detailed storyboard, activities, assessments, facilitation plan and modality-specific design.

🤖 Recommended Agent
Assessment Question Generator

Detailed Design

Activities

  • Expand objectives into detailed learning flow.
  • Build storyboard or detailed design.
  • Design activities, debriefs and practice.
  • Design assessments or checks.
  • Plan facilitator notes and learner materials.

Tools and formats

  • PowerPoint for VLC/VGL or facilitator-led formats.
  • Rise, Storyline or HTML for digital learning.
  • Storyboard and media plan for development support.

Deliverables

  • Detailed design
  • Storyboard
  • Assessment plan
  • Facilitation plan
  • Media plan
Stage 6

Development

Build the learning product and coordinate multimedia and development support.

🤖 Recommended Agent
Style Guide Assistant
QA1 VLC Compliance Checker (optional)

Development

Participants

  • LD / LXD
  • VH / LH support team
  • Graphic designers
  • Video developers
  • SMEs for clarification as needed

Activities

  • Build in the recommended tool.
  • Create interactions, graphics, videos and assessments.
  • Apply brand and accessibility considerations early.
  • Prepare course for alpha review.

Deliverables

  • Working build
  • Course assets
  • Media assets
  • Draft assessments
  • Learner/facilitator materials
Stage 7

Alpha Review

Review the working build with SMEs/SMRs for accuracy, relevance and completeness.

Alpha Review

Participants

  • LD / LXD
  • Lead SMR
  • Relevant SMEs / SMRs
  • LPS for review coordination as needed

Activities

  • Validate accuracy.
  • Identify content gaps.
  • Remove content that does not need to be in the module.
  • Capture feedback and update build.

Deliverables

  • Alpha feedback
  • Content updates
  • Updated build
  • Open items log
Stage 8

Copy Edit

Check brand, style, grammar, copyright, accessibility language and formatting consistency.

🤖 Recommended Agent
Style Guide Assistant

Copy Edit

Activities

  • Apply copy edit guidelines.
  • Check approved/prohibited words.
  • Check punctuation, capitalization and formatting.
  • Check copyright callouts.
  • Review accessibility language.

AI support opportunity

  • Use a style-guide/checklist assistant before formal copy edit.
  • Identify brand or copyright issues early to reduce rework.
  • Human copy editor/reviewer remains final authority.

Deliverables

  • Copy-edited content
  • Clean version
  • Style guide check
  • Copyright clearance
  • Accessibility language review
Stage 9

Gold Review

Final business review and final content signoff before QA and publication preparation.

Gold Review

Participants

  • Business sponsor or lead business contact
  • LD / LXD
  • LPS
  • SME/SMR as needed

Activities

  • Final business validation.
  • Confirm accuracy and alignment.
  • Confirm readiness for QA.
  • Capture final content signoff.

Deliverables

  • Gold signoff
  • Approved build
  • Final approval record
Milestone gate: Final content signoff is required before QA and compliance are completed.

CPE/CE documentation

  • Accessibility approval

Stage 11

Publication

Final setup, publication and learner access confirmation.

Publication

Participants

  • LPS
  • L&D Support / Ops
  • LD / LXD support as needed

Activities

  • Upload and configure course.
  • Complete SuccessFactors / LMS setup.
  • Attach or upload final documentation.
  • Confirm catalog entry and learner access.

Deliverables

  • Published course
  • LMS configuration
  • Catalog entry
  • Final documentation
  • Go-live confirmation

Modality-specific checkpoints

Use these checks during detailed design, development and QA planning.

ILT / In-person

Classroom or in-person learning

  • Facilitation plan and activities.
  • Learner engagement strategy.
  • Timing and logistics.
  • Clear debriefs and key takeaways.
VLC / VGL

Live virtual learning

  • Polling strategy and engagement points.
  • Virtual monitoring plan.
  • Timing and technology requirements.
  • Facilitator instructions.
eLearning / Self-study

Asynchronous learning

  • Knowledge checks and assessments.
  • Feedback quality.
  • Completion rules.
  • Duration and minutes calculation.
Common quality risks

Check early to reduce rework

  • Objectives not achieved.
  • Activities not debriefed.
  • Missing summaries/key takeaways.
  • Documentation changes not applied consistently.
  • Polling or assessment quality issues.

Copy Edit / QA & Compliance: Style Guide Assistant
Context: Use before sending content for formal copy edit or brand review.
Act as Style Guide Assistant.

Review the content below against EY branding, style and copy-edit expectations.

Check for:
- Acronyms not expanded on first use
- Serial comma issues
- Sentence case vs title case issues
- All caps usage
- Straight quotes vs smart quotes
- US English spelling
- Risky words from the watch-out list
- Grammar and punctuation inconsistencies
- Accessibility language concerns
- Copyright-sensitive references

For each issue provide:
- Issue
- Location
- Why it matters
- Suggested fix

Then provide an overall readiness rating:
- Ready
- Ready with minor edits
- Needs significant edits
