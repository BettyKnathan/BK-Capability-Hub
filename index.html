Quick Prompt Recipes

Analyze capability gaps

When to use: Reviewing SME notes, stakeholder inputs or learning needs.

Suggested prompt: Act as a Senior Learning Experience Designer. Review the source material and identify business problems, capability gaps, desired behaviour changes and measurable learning objectives.

Human review: Is training really required, or is the issue workflow, tools, manager support or clarity?

Challenge my assumptions

When to use: High-stakes recommendations, decisions or leadership messages.

Suggested prompt: Do not optimize for agreement. Challenge my assumptions and identify risks, blind spots and counterarguments.

Blank slate analysis

When to use: When you want independent thinking without prior context bias.

Suggested prompt: Use only the information contained in this prompt. Ignore prior context unless explicitly requested.

Core philosophy

AI adoption is fundamentally a behaviour change challenge, not just a training challenge. AI gives me options; I make the decision. Success is not “Did they use AI?” Success is “Would they choose to use it again?”
