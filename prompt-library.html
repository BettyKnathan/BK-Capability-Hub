Prompt Quality Checklist

Role

Who should AI act as?

Specify the role, perspective or expertise you want the output to reflect.

Context

What is the situation?

Explain audience, purpose, background, source material and what success looks like.

Review

What must humans verify?

Check accuracy, confidentiality, tone, brand fit, practicality and judgment before using outputs.

6. Betty's Self-check Coach

Use this section when the output may be persuasive but still needs human discernment.

Before using AI output

Ask better questions

  • What problem am I actually solving?
  • What evidence do I have?
  • What assumptions am I making?
  • What would a skeptic say?
  • What behaviour am I trying to change?
  • How will I know if it worked?
Quality check

Do not skip human review

  • Is this accurate?
  • Is this safe to share?
  • Is this useful for the audience?
  • Is the tone right?
  • What would I change before using it?
  • What should remain human-owned?
Challenge my assumptions
Context: Use before important decisions or recommendations.
Do not optimize for agreement.

1. Risks
2. Blind spots
3. Counterarguments
4. Unintended consequences
5. Evidence I may be missing
6. Stakeholders I may not have considered
7. Conditions under which my recommendation would fail
